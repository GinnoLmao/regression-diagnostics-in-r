# Data provenance and publication boundary

The original coursework referenced local student-performance and employee-satisfaction CSV files. A verifiable public source, redistribution license, data-collection protocol and consent basis were not established. The publication copy does not include those files, their rows, respondent IDs, plots derived from them or original-study regression results.

There is therefore no verified public download link for the original study data. Obtain it only from its legitimate owner with suitable permission, or supply independently authorized data matching the documented schema. Do not treat the files' presence in a coursework folder as redistribution permission.

`tests/synthetic_data.R` creates invented observations solely to exercise the code. It does not draw from, anonymize, statistically match or reconstruct the original CSVs. Synthetic output is prominently labeled and cannot support claims about real students or employees.

The repository is reproducible as a software-method demonstration without private data. The original empirical studies remain unreproduced. No new dataset is silently substituted to obtain better original-study findings.
